Who it's for

Built for specific teams.
Not every team.

Aegis is deliberately designed for three types of organizations. If you're one of them, this replaces your entire compliance stack.

Healthcare IT & SaaS

You handle PHI and need HIPAA documented before your next enterprise deal closes.

Your compliance program lives in Google Drive. Security reviews are three-day scrambles. You need HIPAA and SOC 2 in place without hiring a compliance team or a Big Four firm.

Scale-ups & Mid-market

You've outgrown PandaDoc and spreadsheets but can't justify a $50K AuditBoard contract.

50–300 people, real contracts being signed, real vendor exposure accumulating. You need an actual GRC program — one that doesn't require a six-month implementation or a dedicated compliance hire.

MSPs & vCISOs

You manage compliance for multiple clients and need a multi-tenant platform, not one tool per client.

Super admin controls, tenant isolation, and per-client module configurations. Manage a full compliance program for 10 clients from one dashboard — without 10 separate tool subscriptions.

Security & architecture

Secure by architecture.

A platform that audits your compliance has to clear a higher bar itself. Aegis is built to pass the same reviews it helps you win.

Multi-tenant isolation

Every workspace is logically isolated with scoped access, so one tenant's data is never reachable from another.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with managed keys — from contracts and PHI to your Company Playbook.

Immutable audit logging

Every action is recorded to a tamper-evident trail, giving you defensible evidence for any auditor or regulator.

SOC 2 & HIPAA ready

Built to the controls our customers are held to, with BAAs available for teams handling protected health data.
