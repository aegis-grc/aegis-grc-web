# Homepage visual direction: the three prototypes

Evaluated on the homepage hero and product portfolio only. All three shared the same new foundation
(light-first ground, Geist + Geist Mono, hairline "planes" as abstract product UI, ink primary CTA).
`before-home.png` is the previous dark/serif site for comparison.

| Direction                                | Screenshots                      | Frontend Design                                    | Impeccable                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A**: Precision / minimal enterprise    | `a-1440-1.png`                   | Calmest and fastest to build; risks being too safe | Borderline, leaning template: no product evidence, decorative bars "encode nothing", double hairlines                                             |
| **B**: Modern AI / layered interface     | `b3-1440-1.png`, `b3-1440-2.png` | Recommended: carries the layered-ecosystem idea    | Borderline: best planes, but an overlapping floating-UI collage is "the 2022-24 SaaS hero"; pill filter has no purpose; planes occlude each other |
| **C**: Editorial technology / asymmetric | `c-1440-1.png`, `c-1440-2.png`   | Most memorable; leans editorial                    | Modern in voice but broken as shipped: the plane covers "security." and the CTA; coloured keyword; mirrored tinted bands are a 2018 template      |

## Decision

The two reviews disagreed (Frontend Design: B with C's rhythm; Impeccable: C with A and B parts), so the
selected direction is an explicit hybrid:

- **From C:** very large, tight Geist headline; verb-led product headings ("Govern risk." / "Grow revenue." /
  "Defend operations."); product moments that differ in scale and structure instead of mirroring.
- **From B:** the product-specific planes and the dark Sentinel plane, but recomposed as an asymmetric orthogonal
  cluster with a small Aria hub and spokes in the gaps, so nothing occludes anything.
- **From A:** Aria as a recurring system element (lane / bus / approval-gate diagram) and hairline rows for index pages.
- **Dropped:** the segmented pill control, empty "Owner" column, decorative bars, coloured headline keyword, the
  floating shield, and the wide-tracked "AEGIS" wordmark (replaced by a solid mark and a tighter "Aegis" wordmark).

The prototype pages themselves were throwaway and are not in the repository.
